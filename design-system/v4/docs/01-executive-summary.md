# Executive summary

Audit of https://avocatapp.com/ (12 public pages, fetched 11 Nov 2026) and `andreslevano/avocatapp_legaltech_v3` @ `c5d8521` (27 Apr 2026). Logged-in screens were **not** seen; app findings come from the code and are marked as inferred.

1. **The brand direction is right; the implementation isn't a system yet.** Cream + near-black + gold + Garamond reads as trust and craft for lawyers and stays approachable for laypeople. But v3 tokens are bypassed: **58 distinct hex values**, **489 raw hex literals**, **360 arbitrary colour classes**, **504 Tailwind default palette classes**, **31 font sizes** (15 of them arbitrary `text-[Npx]`) and **22 distinct button styles** across 224 raw `<button>`s (the `ui/Button` component is used 44 times; `ui/Card` 0 times).

2. **Several pairs fail WCAG AA, and two are effectively invisible.** Gold `#B8882A` on cream is 2.91:1; white on gold (the gold CTA and the user's chat bubble) 3.19:1; the untokenised muted `#6b6050` (89 uses) 2.77:1 on the dark card; placeholders `#9a9a9a` 2.57:1 on cream, and `#3a3630` in the agent input **1.42:1**. Focus styling is mostly removed (`outline-none` ×50, `focus-visible` ×4). v4 fixes every pair (106 checked, 0 failures) without changing the palette's character — accessible gold for text is the existing active state `#8c6920` family.

3. **There is one shell component but two visual systems inside it, plus real bugs.** `AppShellClient` wraps both `(app)/*` and `dashboard/*`, but legacy pages paint cream/white panels with near-black text inside the dark frame; `/profile` and `/subscription` use a third, light shell. **`bg-surface-muted` is used 259 times and is not defined** — those backgrounds render transparent. `Logo variant="signature"` (footer) loads a **blue `#2563EB` “A”** and forces it gold with a CSS filter.

4. **Trust and compliance are the biggest product risk.** Copy promises documents “listos para presentar ante el juzgado” and use “sin conocimientos jurídicos”, but the UI shows **no AI disclaimer**, citations are plain text with no verification, there is **no aviso legal / company identification** (LSSI-CE art. 10), cookies are consented “by continuing to browse” while Google Ads and Microsoft Clarity load unconditionally, and the privacy policy lacks legal bases per purpose, retention periods, named processors and a DPO or dedicated privacy contact. Trust badges (“GDPR Compliant”, “Cifrado bancario”, “99.9% Uptime”) have no evidence behind them.

5. **Pricing and numbers contradict each other across pages.** Autoservicio is “€50/mes” and “pago por documento, sin suscripción”; “Solo escritos €10” exists on the home page but not at signup; cases are “1,200+” or “100+”, time saved 85 % or 80 %, study areas 8 or “10+”. Navigation, footers and social accounts differ between the home and the legal pages (legacy `Header`/`Footer` still in use, with dead `#caracteristicas`/`#precios` anchors).

6. **v4 delivers:** a gold 50–950 and warm neutral 0–950 ramp that absorbs every v3 value; 31 semantic roles in two themes; 8 legal status tokens (incl. *próximo*, an intentional addition the code already paints amber); a full type scale including a legal-document style that replaces Times New Roman/Arial in previews and Helvetica in the generated PDFs; one radius, shadow, z-index and motion scale; Lucide replacing 320 emoji; 56 documented components with live previews; five before/after screens; and drop-in `tailwind.config.js`, `globals.css`, `tokens.ts` plus a ratcheting CI guard.

7. **Do first (each under a day):** ship the v4 token files with v3 aliases re-pointed (defines the missing `surface-muted` and gives every element a visible focus ring and legible placeholder), add the AI disclaimer + footer legal identification + a real consent banner, unify the public Navbar/Footer, publish one `PLANS` constant for every price, and turn on the CI guard with a baseline. Then migrate the agent, dashboard and wizard flows onto `(app)` components and delete the legacy shell.
